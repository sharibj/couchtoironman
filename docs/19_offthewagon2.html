Fell off the wagon (AGAIN!)

Completing the Half Marathon was a big achievement for me. Maybe that's why I thought I "deserve" a break. This notion of "deserving a break" is a scam. You deserve a celebration. A pat on the back. Recovery. Never a break. I should've kept the momentum going. Instead, I stopped. I had a year to prepare for the marathon but 6 months went by in a blink. Instead of progressing I regressed.

No running for 6 months. No swimming either. The instructor went on a holiday and then got out of touch. I continued my strength training but without much progression.

Now I have 5 months to prepare. Better late than never. I'll start running again. I might be the last person to cross the finish line (again) but I intend to cross it.
